Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Data processed

The Repère publishing tool only processes data of the Repère TikTok account that authorized it: account identifier and display name (to know where to post), access tokens provided by TikTok, and statistics of videos published by this account (views, likes, comments, shares). No data about other TikTok users is collected.

Purpose

This data is used only to publish videos approved by the editor and to measure their performance in order to improve future videos.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted and never published. Statistics are kept as long as the Repère account exists. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Your rights

The authorization can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security, Manage app permissions). For any access or deletion request: repere.actus@gmail.com.
